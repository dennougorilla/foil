# FOIL v0.14.1

## New

- **Open all (#43).** The pack shop has an **Open all** button under Open pack: tap it twice and every sealed pack on the tray opens at once, without the openings, and the haul shows as one list, a row per pack. Each opening can still be watched from the shop.
- **Packs reorganized (#43).** A new **Jewel** pack (Crystal, Opal, Raden, Kintsugi), and the Supporter pack is now the celebration pack (Confetti, Snow Globe, Fireworks). Finishes you own stay yours; a pack that gained a finish is sealed again and says what it adds.
- **Edit deck opens on All (#43).** One list, your hand first, then by family, with a pip for each card's pack; the tabs still narrow to one pack.
- **Smoother on low-priced Android phones (#31).** One quality table for the whole page steps the card, the backdrop and the sparks down on a slow device and climbs back when it can. Add `?fps=1` to the address for a small frame-rate meter.

## Fixed

- Free placement: the print menu and the yellow guides no longer stay on screen or follow the page when it scrolls (#49).
- Edit deck: the tabs have room above the cards, and scrolled cards fade out beneath them instead of being cut on a hard line.
