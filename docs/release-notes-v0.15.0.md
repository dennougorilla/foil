# FOIL v0.15.0

## New

- **Three new finishes.** **Chameleon** (color-shift car paint that flows from violet to gold as the card tilts) joins the Metal pack; **Rainy Window** (the picture behind fogged, rainy glass you can wipe with a finger) and **Marble** (suminagashi ink rings that swirl after your finger) join the Nature pack. 36 finishes in all. Finishes you own stay yours; Metal and Nature are sealed again and say what they add.
- **Backdrops (#44).** Fine-tune → Card → Backdrop: Swirl, Felt, Studio, Velvet, Bokeh, Stars, Confetti, Plain (your color) or Transparent. The GIF, APNG and MP4 now show the same backdrop, moving with the loop. Transparent makes the GIF and APNG clear; an MP4 has no transparency, so it comes out black.
- **Use as the whole card (#45).** A picture that already is a card (frame and words drawn in) can be the card itself: no FOIL frame, nameplate or message, in the picture's own proportions, with only the finish on it. Pixel art is enlarged with hard pixels.

## Changed

- **Wording (#38).** A pass over the interface texts in both languages, with one name for each thing (`docs/glossary.md`); the Balatro credit is one line.
